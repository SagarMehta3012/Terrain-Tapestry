01 — Field Position

The layers of Terrain Tapestry

Rooted in research and driven by curiosity, this platform studies how spaces influence human experience — from planting palettes to the evolving role of urban greens.

Landscape architecture is a dynamic discipline, shaped equally by creativity, ecology and design innovation. Each category on this journal offers a different lens on that discipline — engineering, ecological transformation, urban fault lines, port regeneration, waterfronts, place-naming, avifauna, play and pedagogy — held together by one belief.

That the built and the natural are never really separate systems — only different tempos of the same terrain.

04 — The Writer

About Resham Mehta

RESHAM MEHTA — EDITOR & WRITER, TERRAIN TAPESTRY

Resham Mehta writes and researches at the intersection of landscape architecture, ecology and the built environment. Terrain Tapestry began as The Architecture of Landscapes, a focused study of landscape's structural and engineering dimensions, and has since grown into a wider inquiry spanning hydrology, geology, planting design, urban ecology and the cultural histories embedded in place.

The work is deliberately cross-disciplinary — treating a harbour's silt, a city's fault lines, a street's place name and a child's play space as parts of one continuous terrain, rather than separate fields of study.

  • Publication Terrain Tapestry, formerly The Architecture of Landscapes
  • Focus Landscape architecture, ecology, hydrology & urban research
  • Based in Mumbai, India
